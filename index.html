📖 How to Play

Objective: Find all matching emoji pairs before the timer runs out.

  • Click any face-down card to reveal its emoji.
  • Flip a second card. If they match, they stay visible.
  • If they don't match, they flip back over.

Level Progression Rules

  • 1️⃣
    Classic: Standard memory matching.
  • 2️⃣
    Preview: You get a 2-second peek at all cards before starting.
  • 3️⃣
    Shuffle: When half the pairs are matched, all unmatched cards shuffle positions.
  • 4️⃣
    Ultimate: Preview + Shuffle + Mistake Limit. Make too many wrong guesses and it's Game Over!
⚠️ The timer only starts after you click your first card!
